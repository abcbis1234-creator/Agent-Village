## Craft rules (every chapter)

- Open on motion or unease within two sentences.
- One concrete problem, one emotional beat, one escalation.
- End on a hook that makes Chapter N+1 mandatory.
- Never explain what a scene can show.
- Keep paragraphs short; let dialogue carry tension.
