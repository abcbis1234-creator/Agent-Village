## §2 — Research-Driven Upgrades Applied to the Story

### §2.1 Premise (research-informed)

The Disaster Realm is a wound in the world; Kael is the only person alive who can suture it. Exile is quarantine; the kingdom feared a zero-mana keeper would restore what they could not bear to remember.

Applied research upgrades:
- Sharpened hook for legendary positioning: "They exiled the only man who could repair the end of the world — and called it mercy."
- Anchored the Codification system in the Lord of Mysteries model: mystery + evolving system.

### §2.2 Codex / Restoration System (research-informed)

Applied upgrades:
- Calibration = the realm's wound tightening/loosening with each restoration.
- Restoration requires keeper + cost.
- Codex is arbiter/ledger, never an actor (Mother of Learning: knowledge as power; Lord of Mysteries: mystery).

### §2.3 Mystery Engine (research-informed)

Applied upgrades:
- Seven-pointed star → legendary mystery (Tower of God / Lord of Mysteries Fool).
- Open gate vision → mystery origin.
- Beacon chain → hope infrastructure / escalating stakes.
- Ledger → living record.
- Larger presence → overarching force (Omniscient Reader / Tower of God).

### §2.4 Character Arcs (research-informed)
