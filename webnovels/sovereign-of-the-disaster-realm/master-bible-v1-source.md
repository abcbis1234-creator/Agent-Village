# Sovereign of the Disaster Realm — Master Bible

Version 1.0 - Compiled from Chapters 1-11 and approved canon discussions.

## Series Premise

Kael Arden, a repairman with zero mana, is summoned to Aurelia, rejected by the kingdom, and
exiled into the Disaster Realm. Rather than becoming a conqueror, he becomes a keeper, restorer,
and builder.

## Core Themes

Responsibility not ownership; restoration over destruction; maintenance as heroism; community; no
one carries a weight alone.

## Main Characters

Kael Arden: practical builder and future Sovereign Candidate. Lyra: watchpost keeper and beacon
tender. Leon: Hero-Class summon with Radiant affinity.

## Major Mysteries

The seven-pointed star, the open gate vision, the beacon chain, the ledger language, the Codex,
the missing keepers, the vanished hounds, the larger presence.

## Codex Principles

Learn, restore, preserve, record. Restoration advances calibration. The Codex does not solve
problems automatically.

## Arkhaven Ethic

No one carries a weight alone.

## Chapter Canon (1-11)

1 The Summoning

Kael and Leon are summoned. Leon receives Hero-Class status. Kael receives Mana Capacity 0
and is exiled.

2 The Ancient Road

Kael enters the Disaster Realm and discovers the Old Road and seven-point milestone.

3 Footprints of the Lost

Verge-Hounds appear. Kael survives and discovers evidence of previous inhabitants.

4 Lyra

Kael meets Lyra and discovers the watchpost and broken gate.

5 The Open Gate

Kael and Lyra rebuild the gate and survive the hounds.

6 Foundations

Settlement planning begins. Beacon chain discovered.

7 Shared Fire

Partnership deepens. Warm ledger stone is discovered.

8 First Fall

Ledger identified. Storm approaches.

9 The Storm

Stair destroyed, fire extinguished, gate survives.

10 The Codex

Sovereign Candidate confirmed. Codex awakens.

11 Restore

First Codex-guided restoration. Unknown traveller detected.
